# 🔮 Kronos AI - Future Product Roadmap & Enhancements

## Phase 1: Authentication & Integration
- [ ] **Google & LinkedIn OAuth 2.0 Integration**: 1-click social sign-in alongside Nodemailer OTP verification.
- [ ] **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) support via Authenticator apps.

## Phase 2: Browser Extension & Auto-Filling
- [ ] **Kronos Chrome Extension**: Chrome Extension allowing 1-click Easy Apply auto-filling on external job portals.
- [ ] **LinkedIn Recruiter InMail Automation**: Automated messaging queue for targeted recruiter outreach.

## Phase 3: Advanced AI Capabilities
- [ ] **Real-Time Voice AI Interview Simulator**: Interactive mock interview practice using Web Speech API & Real-time AI voice generation.
- [ ] **Resume PDF Export & Dynamic Styling**: Export ATS-optimized resumes in custom PDF formats.

## Phase 4: Enterprise & Team Analytics
- [ ] **Multi-Tenant Team Workspaces**: Collaborative application tracking for career counselors and placement cells.
- [ ] **Advanced Salary & Market Insights**: Real-time market salary benchmark charts per target role and location.
